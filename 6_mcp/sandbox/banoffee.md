# Banoffee Pie Recipe

## Overview
An easy family favorite with buttery pastry and sweet dulce de leche. Served best with a generous dollop of cream.

## Ingredients

### For the Pastry
- 100g chilled butter (plus extra for greasing)
- 200g plain flour
- 1 medium egg (separated)
- 1 tbsp golden caster sugar

### For the Filling & Topping
- 4 bananas, sliced
- 394g caramel or dulce de leche
- 300ml double cream
- Dark chocolate (optional, for garnish)

## Method

1. **Make the pastry case:**
   - Put butter and flour in a food processor and pulse until it resembles fresh breadcrumbs.
   - Add the egg yolk and sugar, pulse again.
   - Add very cold water a tablespoon at a time, pulse until dough comes together.
   
2. **Knead dough:**
   - Remove dough and knead gently to smooth ball.
   - Wrap in cling film and chill in the fridge for 30 minutes.

3. **Prepare tart tin:**
   - Heat oven to 190C/170C fan/gas 5.
   - Grease a 23cm loose-bottomed tart tin.
   - Roll out pastry and line the tin leaving half a cm over the edge.
   - Snip pastry edges with scissors.

4. **Blind bake pastry:**
   - Line pastry with parchment and baking beans or uncooked rice.
   - Bake 15 minutes.
   - Remove paper and beans, brush with egg white.
   - Bake for another 15-20 minutes until crisp and golden.
   - Cool completely.

5. **Assemble the pie:**
   - Spread half the caramel on the pastry base.
   - Layer bananas on top.
   - Cover with remaining caramel.
   - Chill in the fridge.

6. **Final touch:**
   - Whip the cream until thick and spoon over the tart.
   - Finely grate dark chocolate over the top for garnish if desired.

## Notes
- Can be made a day ahead.
- Best served freshly made, cream added on the day of serving.
- Pastry case can be frozen but not once filled.

Enjoy your delicious Banoffee Pie!
